## 1. Scope
This policy covers qru-online.com (the public QRU Online bookstore). It does not cover the
private QRU Factory, which is not accessible to the public.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Browsing:** No account or sign-in is required to browse. We do not use advertising trackers
  and we do not sell personal data.
- **Purchases:** When you buy an ebook we retain an order record: the title purchased, amount,
  currency, the Stripe transaction/session reference, timestamps, and download-link usage count.
  We do **not** collect or store payment card numbers.
- **Payment data (via Stripe):** Name, email, and payment/billing details are collected and
  processed by Stripe, Inc. (our PCI-compliant processor). See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
- **Technical/server logs:** Standard request logs (IP, user agent, timestamps) for security and
  reliability. [Confirm log retention window with hosting/Emergent.]

## 3. How we use information
Fulfilling and delivering your purchase; providing customer support; preventing fraud and abuse
of download links; meeting legal/accounting obligations. We do not use your data for advertising.
