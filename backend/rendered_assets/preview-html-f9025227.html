Slide 1: Understanding Sleep and Rest

SLIDE 3/6
  • **Sleep is not “doing nothing.”**
  • It is active recovery for the brain and body.
  • Sleep supports thinking, mood, immunity, metabolism, and physical repair
  • Rest is valuable, but it is not the same as sleep
  • Better sleep habits can support daily functioning and safety
  • **QRU Key Message:**
  • **Sleep is biological maintenance. Rest is helpful recovery. Both matter.**

Slide 3: Sleep Is Active Maintenance

SLIDE 5/6
  • Sleep supports essential body systems:
  • **Brain function:** learning, memory processing, and attention
  • **Mood regulation:** emotional balance and stress response
  • **Immune function:** normal immune system activity
  • **Heart and metabolic health:** blood pressure, blood sugar, and appetite regulation
  • **Physical recovery:** tissue repair, growth processes, and energy restoration
  • **Plain-Language Explanation:**
  • Sleep is like overnight maintenance for the body.
