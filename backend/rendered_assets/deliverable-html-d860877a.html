Vocabulary

  • Hydration — Having enough fluid in the body to support normal functions like circulation, temperature control, digestion, kidney filtering, and cell activity.
  • Dehydration — A state where the body does not have enough fluid, which can cause fatigue, reduced performance, and poorer attention, mood, or thinking.
  • Electrolyte imbalance — An unsafe disruption in minerals such as sodium that help control fluid balance, which can rarely happen from excessive fluid intake.
  • Cellular metabolism — The chemical work cells do to use energy and keep the body functioning.
