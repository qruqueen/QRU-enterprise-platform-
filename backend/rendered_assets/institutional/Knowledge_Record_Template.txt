Introduction  
The  Purpose  of  the  QRU  Knowledge  Record™  
The  QRU  Knowledge  Record™  is  the  fundamental  building  block  of  the  QRU  Knowledge  
Database™.
 
It  is  not  simply  a  document.  
It  is  a  structured  container  designed  to  transform:  
Information  
↓  
Understanding  

↓  
Application  
↓  
Human  Growth  
Every  QRU  Knowledge  Record™  captures:  
●  What  we  are  trying  to  understand  ●  What  is  true  ●  Why  it  matters  ●  How  it  connects  ●  How  it  can  be  taught  ●  How  it  can  be  applied  ●  How  it  can  improve  over  time
