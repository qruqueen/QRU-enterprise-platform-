Section  1  
Definition  of  a  QRU  Knowledge  Record™  

A  QRU  Knowledge  Record™  is:  
A  structured,  verified,  expandable  intelligence  asset  designed  to  transform  information  into  
understanding
 
and
 
application.
 
It  is  not  simply  notes.  
It  is  not  simply  research.  
It  is  a  reusable  understanding  engine.  
 
Section  2  
Knowledge  Record  Identity™  
Every  record  receives:  
Record  ID  
Example:  
QRU-KR-00001  
 
Title  
Clear  understanding  topic.  
 
Core  Question™  
The  question  this  record  answers.  
Example:  
“How  does  investing  work?”  

 
Audience  
Who  needs  this  understanding?  
Examples:  
●  Children  ●  Students  ●  Parents  ●  Entrepreneurs  ●  Professionals  
 
Version  History  
Tracks:  
●  Creation  ●  Updates  ●  Improvements  
 
Section  3  
QRU  36-Layer  Intelligence  Manufacturing  
Structure™
 
Each  Knowledge  Record  moves  through  layered  development.  
 
Layer  Group  1  

Understanding  Foundation™  
1.  Question  Definition  
What  are  we  trying  to  understand?  
2.  Problem  Identification  
Why  does  this  matter?  
3.  Context  Mapping  
Where  does  this  exist  in  the  world?  
4.  Vocabulary  Foundation  
What  words  must  be  understood?  
 
Layer  Group  2  
Research  Intelligence™  
5.  Source  Collection  
Gather  information.  
6.  Source  Evaluation  
Assess  reliability.  
7.  Evidence  Mapping  
Connect  claims  to  support.  

8.  Research  Summary  
Capture  findings.  
 
Layer  Group  3  
Verification  Architecture™  
9.  Accuracy  Review  
Is  it  correct?  
10.  Context  Review  
Is  it  fairly  represented?  
11.  Bias  Review  
Are  limitations  identified?  
12.  Verification  Approval  
Ready  for  development.  
 
Layer  Group  4  
Translation  Intelligence™  
13.  Simplification  

Remove  unnecessary  complexity.  
14.  Explanation  
Create  clear  understanding.  
15.  Analogy  Creation  
Connect  to  familiar  ideas.  
16.  Story  Development  
Make  meaning  memorable.  
 
Layer  Group  5  
Teaching  Architecture™  
17.  Learning  Objectives  
What  should  someone  know?  
18.  Conversation  Pathway  
How  should  understanding  develop?  
19.  Practice  Application  
How  can  it  be  used?  
20.  Assessment  Design  
How  do  we  measure  understanding?  
 

Layer  Group  6  
Connection  Intelligence™  
21.  Related  Concepts  
What  connects  to  this?  
22.  Real-World  Examples  
Where  does  this  appear?  
23.  Cross-Discipline  Links  
What  other  fields  connect?  
24.  Future  Implications  
Why  will  this  matter?  
 
Layer  Group  7  
Product  Manufacturing™  
25.  Product  Opportunities  
Possible  outputs:  
●  Book  ●  Course  ●  Video  ●  Workbook  ●  Assessment  

26.  Audience  Adaptations  
How  does  it  change  for  different  learners?  
27.  Media  Opportunities  
How  can  it  be  communicated?  
28.  Marketing  Translation  
Why  would  someone  value  it?  
 
Layer  Group  8  
Evolution  &  Improvement™  
29.  Feedback  Collection  
What  did  users  say?  
30.  Performance  Review  
How  did  it  perform?  
31.  Knowledge  Updates  
What  changed?  
32.  Version  Improvement  
How  does  it  get  better?  
 

Layer  Group  9  
Legacy  Intelligence™  
33.  Long-Term  Value  
Will  this  matter  later?  
34.  Generational  Impact  
Can  it  help  future  learners?  
35.  Preservation  Standard  
Should  it  be  maintained?  
36.  Treasure  Approval  
Does  it  meet  QRU  standards?  
 
Section  4  
Knowledge  Record  Quality  Gates™  
Every  record  must  pass:  
 
Gate  1:  
Truth  
Is  it  accurate?  

 
Gate  2:  
Understanding  
Can  humans  grasp  it?  
 
Gate  3:  
Application  
Can  it  improve  decisions  or  actions?  
 
Gate  4:  
Experience  
Can  it  become  a  meaningful  learning  experience?  
 
Gate  5:  
Legacy  
Is  it  worthy  of  preservation?

Section  6  
Knowledge  Record  Governance™  
Every  record  maintains:

Section  7  
Knowledge  Record  Gold  Standard™  
A  complete  Knowledge  Record™  should  answer:  
What  is  it?  
Understanding.  
Why  does  it  matter?  
Purpose.  
How  does  it  work?  
Mechanism.  
Where  does  it  apply?  
Connection.  
What  should  someone  do  with  it?  
Action.  
 

Section  8  
Knowledge  Record  Factory  Rule™  
No  product  should  begin  with:  
“What  should  we  make?”  
Every  product  begins  with:  
“What  understanding  should  exist  first?”
