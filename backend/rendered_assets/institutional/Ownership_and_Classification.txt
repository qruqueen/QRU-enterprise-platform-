Introduction  
The  Purpose  of  the  QRU  Intellectual  Property  Registry™  
As  QRU™  grows,  the  value  of  the  organization  will  exist  across  multiple  asset  categories:  
●  Brand  identity  ●  Methodologies  ●  Knowledge  systems  ●  Databases  ●  Educational  products  ●  Artificial  intelligence  workflows  ●  Standards  ●  Research  infrastructure  
The  QRU  Intellectual  Property  Registry™  exists  to  identify,  organize,  and  protect  these  assets.  
Its  purpose:  

“Know  what  QRU  creates,  understand  why  it  matters,  and  preserve  the  systems  that  create  
future
 
value.”
 
 
Section  1  
QRU  Intellectual  Property  Philosophy™  
QRU  recognizes  four  forms  of  strategic  value:  
1.  Identity  Assets  
Who  QRU  is.  
Examples:  
●  Brand  ●  Mission  ●  Names  ●  Symbols  ●  Language  
 
2.  Method  Assets  
How  QRU  creates  understanding.  
Examples:  
●  Frameworks  ●  Processes  ●  Teaching  systems  ●  Quality  standards  
 
3.  Intelligence  Assets  

What  QRU  accumulates  over  time.  
Examples:  
●  Knowledge  Records™  ●  Databases  ●  Research  ●  Learning  insights  
 
4.  Ecosystem  Assets  
What  QRU  builds  around  the  mission.  
Examples:  
●  Partnerships  ●  Communities  ●  Educator  networks  ●  Future  platforms  
 
Section  2  
QRU  Asset  Classification  System™  
Every  QRU  asset  receives  a  classification.  
 
Class  A  
Public  Brand  Assets™  
Purpose:  

Create  recognition  and  trust.  
Examples:  
●  QRU™  ●  Quest  for  Real  Understanding™  ●  QRU  visual  identity  ●  Public  mission  statements  ●  Public  educational  messages  
Protection  Considerations:  
Trademark  review  recommended.  
 
Class  B  
Protected  Methodology  Assets™  
Purpose:  
Protect  how  QRU  transforms  information  into  understanding.  
Examples:  
●  QRU  Knowledge  Architecture™  36-Layer  Intelligence  Manufacturing  Standard™  ●  QRU  Universal  Production  Formula™  ●  QRU  Conversation-First™  Teaching  Methodology  ●  QRU  Guided  Understanding  System™  ●  QRU  Understanding  Transformation  Engine™  ●  QRU  Treasure  Standard™  
Protection  Considerations:  
Documentation,  trademark  review,  controlled  publication.  
 
Class  C  

Internal  Operating  Assets™  
Purpose:  
Protect  the  Factory  operating  system.  
Examples:  
●  QRU  AI  Factory™  Pipeline  ●  Production  workflows  ●  Agent  instructions  ●  Internal  review  systems  ●  Manufacturing  procedures  
Protection  Considerations:  
Internal  documentation  and  trade  secret  management.  
 
Class  D  
Strategic  Intelligence  Assets™  
Purpose:  
Create  long-term  compounding  value.  
Examples:  
●  QRU  Knowledge  Database™  ●  QRU  Knowledge  Graph™  ●  Living  Intelligence  Library™  ●  Research  archives  ●  Assessment  data  ●  Product  performance  insights  
Protection  Considerations:  
Access  control,  backup  systems,  documentation.  

 
Class  E  
Product  Assets™  
Purpose:  
Deliver  QRU  value  to  customers.  
Examples:  
●  Books  ●  Workbooks  ●  Courses  ●  Videos  ●  Presentations  ●  Teacher  resources  ●  Learning  tools  
Protection  Considerations:  
Copyright  registration  where  appropriate.

Section  4  
QRU  Trade  Secret  Protection  Principles™  
Internal  assets  should  be  evaluated  by:  
Value  
Does  this  create  competitive  advantage?  
Difficulty  
Would  recreation  require  significant  time  or  resources?  
Confidentiality  
Is  this  information  publicly  available?  
Strategic  Importance  
Does  this  strengthen  QRU’s  future?
