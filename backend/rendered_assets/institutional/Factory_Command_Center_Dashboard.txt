Purpose  
The  QRU  Factory  Command  Center  Dashboard™  provides  a  centralized  operating  view  of  all  
QRU
 
creation
 
activities.
 
Its  purpose  is  to  transform  a  complex  creative  ecosystem  into  a  measurable,  organized  
production
 
environment.
 
The  Command  Center  answers:  
●  What  are  we  creating?  ●  Why  are  we  creating  it?  ●  Where  is  it  in  the  process?  ●  What  quality  standards  have  been  achieved?  ●  What  decisions  are  required?  
 
Founder  Principle™  
“A  factory  cannot  improve  what  it  cannot  see.”
