Section  1  
The  QRU  Factory  Mission™  
The  Factory  exists  to  answer  one  question:  
“How  do  we  remove  as  much  unnecessary  confusion  as  possible  without  losing  truth?”  
Every  QRU  creation  must  serve  this  mission.
