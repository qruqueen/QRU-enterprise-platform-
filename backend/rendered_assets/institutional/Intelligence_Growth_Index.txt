Introduction  
The  Purpose  of  the  QRU  Intelligence  Growth  Index™  
Traditional  education  often  measures:  
●  Completion  ●  Memorization  ●  Test  scores  ●  Participation  
QRU  measures  something  deeper:  
Understanding  Growth™  
The  QRU  Intelligence  Growth  Index™  exists  to  evaluate  whether  a  learner  has  moved  from:  
Information  

↓  
Awareness  
↓  
Understanding  
↓  
Application  
↓  
Wisdom  
↓  
Growth  
 
Section  1  
The  QRU  Measurement  Philosophy™  
QRU  believes:  
Information  asks:  
“What  do  you  know?”  
Understanding  asks:  
“What  can  you  explain?”  
Application  asks:  
“What  can  you  do?”  
Wisdom  asks:  
“How  do  you  use  what  you  know  responsibly?”  

The  purpose  of  measurement  is  not  to  judge  people.  
The  purpose  is  to  reveal  growth  opportunities.

Section  3  
QRU  Understanding  Growth  Scale™  
Level  1  —  Exposure  
The  learner  encounters  new  information.  
Question:  
“Have  they  seen  it?”  
 
Level  2  —  Recognition  
The  learner  identifies  concepts.  
Question:  
“Do  they  recognize  it?”  
 
Level  3  —  Understanding  
The  learner  explains  meaning.  
Question:  
“Can  they  describe  it?”  
 
Level  4  —  Application  
The  learner  uses  knowledge.  
Question:  
“Can  they  apply  it?”  

 
Level  5  —  Teaching  
The  learner  transfers  understanding.  
Question:  
“Can  they  help  someone  else  understand?”  
 
Level  6  —  Wisdom  
The  learner  applies  understanding  responsibly.  
Question:  
“Can  they  use  knowledge  for  meaningful  decisions?”  
 
Section  4  
QRU  Assessment  Framework™  
Every  QRU  assessment  should  include:  
Question  
What  are  we  measuring?  
 
Understanding  Check  
Can  the  learner  explain?  
 

Connection  Check  
Can  the  learner  relate  it  to  life?  
 
Application  Check  
Can  the  learner  use  it?  
 
Reflection  Check  
What  changed  because  of  this  understanding?
