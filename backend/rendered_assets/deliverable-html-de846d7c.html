Multiplication, Made Understandable

Multiplication means:

> **Many groups combined.**

Example:

A basketball player makes **8 free throws** at each practice.

They practice **5 times**.

So:

> **8 × 5 = 40 free throws**

Now math is not just a worksheet problem.

It becomes a way to track progress.
