    2. What Is Day Trading?

    SLIDE 3/13
    • Buying and selling financial instruments within a short time period, often within the same day.
    • It combines market analysis, timing decisions, risk management, emotional control, and process discipline.

    3. Why Market Understanding Matters

    SLIDE 4/13

    6. Risk and Uncertainty

    SLIDE 7/13
    • No one can predict every market movement.
    • Risk management exists because uncertainty is real.
    • The goal is informed decision-making, not guaranteed success.

    7. Trading Psychology

    SLIDE 8/13
    • Humans make decisions, and emotions influence behavior.
    • Fear of missing out can lead to unplanned decisions.
    • Discipline helps protect the process.

    8. The Pilot Analogy

    SLIDE 9/13
    • A pilot cannot control the weather.
    • A pilot controls preparation, training, instruments, and response.
    • A trader cannot control the market.
    • A trader controls education, process, risk, and decisions.

    9. Reflection and Improvement

    SLIDE 10/13
    • A good decision can still produce an unfavorable outcome.

    Presentation Script

    SLIDE 12/13
    • Welcome to QRU Day Trading A-Z Academy.
    • That process includes five major parts: knowledge, analysis, decision, risk, and reflection.
    • First is knowledge. A person must understand the system. What is a market? What is an asset? What does volatility mean? What is liquidity? These ideas create the foundation for financial literacy.
    • Second is analysis. Analysis means interpreting information. A trader studies market conditions, price movement, and context. Analysis does not remove uncertainty, but it helps a person think more clearly.
    • Third is decision. A decision should come from a plan, not from impulse. One of the most common mistakes is entering a position because of fear of missing out. That ignores planning and emotional discipline.
    • This is the heart of financial intelligence. It is not the ability to know what will happen next. It is the ability to understand systems, manage uncertainty, and make better decisions.
    • Remember this sentence: Trading is not about controlling the market. It is about controlling the quality of your decisions.

    Vocabulary

    SLIDE 13/13
    • **Day trading** — The practice of buying and selling financial instruments within a short time period, often within the same trading day.
    • **Asset** — Something that has value, such as stocks, currencies, or commodities.
    • **Market** — A place or system where buyers and sellers exchange value.
    • **Volatility** — How much and how quickly prices move in a market.
    • **Liquidity** — How easily something can be bought or sold.
    • **Strategy** — A planned method for making decisions.
    • **Risk management** — Protecting resources by planning for uncertainty.
    • **Probability** — The study of possible outcomes and how likely they are.
