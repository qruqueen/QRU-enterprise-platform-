AI Literacy — Book

Deep Roots™

AI literacy grows from the need to understand systems that use data and algorithms to support or automate tasks. Modern AI often relies on machine learning, where models identify patterns in data, but those models can carry uncertainty, reflect bias, or produce inaccurate outputs. This is why technical understanding must be paired with ethical reasoning and critical evaluation. As AI becomes part of institutions and public life, AI literacy connects computer science, social responsibility, workplace readiness, and civic participation.
