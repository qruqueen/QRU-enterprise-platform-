Short Video Production Script

QRU Factory Acceptance Test — Understanding Sleep and Rest

Topic: Why humans need sleep — and why rest cannot fully replace it

Length: ~90 seconds

Style: Warm, clear, educational, visually clean

Audience: General public

Core Message: Rest can pause the drain, but sleep does the repair.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this short video, viewers should understand:

1. Rest and sleep are both valuable, but they are not the same.

2. Sleep is an active biological process that supports brain, body, mood, and safety.

3. Most adults need at least 7 hours of sleep, while needs vary by age and individual.

4. Simple sleep habits can support healthier sleep.

5. Persistent insomnia, excessive daytime sleepiness, loud snoring, gasping, or suspected sleep apnea should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

6. This video is educational and is not a substitute for medical advice.

Accessibility and Production Requirements

  • Tone: Supportive and educational; avoid fear-based or shame-based messaging.
  • Medical caution: Keep all health guidance non-diagnostic and encourage professional support for persistent or concerning symptoms.
