Activity 12

A. Major Financial Risks

  • Prices can move against you quickly. A trade that looks safe can become a loss in seconds or minutes.
  • Borrowed money can increase both gains and losses. With margin, you may lose more than your original deposit, face margin calls, or be forced to sell positions at a bad time.
  • Even when a platform advertises low or no commissions, trading may still involve:
  • Bid-ask spreads
  • Exchange fees
  • Payment for order flow effects
  • Slippage
  • Data fees

  • Faith and values can shape how a person handles money, stress, honesty, and responsibility. For many people, faith encourages humility, patience, generosity, and care for others.
Activity 15

A. What Faith Can Do

  • Faith or deeply held values may help you:
  • Tell the truth about losses
  • Avoid secrecy
  • Treat money as a responsibility
  • Resist greed
  • Practice patience
  • Ask for help
  • Care for family needs first
Activity 16

B. What Faith Cannot Do

  • Faith cannot:
  • Predict whether a stock, option, crypto asset, currency, or futures contract will rise or fall
  • Guarantee profit
  • Remove market risk
  • Make leverage safe
  • Protect you from poor decisions
  • Turn a risky trade into a wise trade
  • Prove that a financial impulse is spiritually approved
Activity 17

C. Important Boundary

  • A feeling of peace, confidence, urgency, or “confirmation” should never replace risk management, professional guidance, written limits, or honesty with loved ones.
  • > **Faith can guide your values, but it cannot predict the market.**

Activity 21

B. Strong Stop Signal

  • If you checked any of the following, consider pausing trading immediately and seeking support:
  • Hiding losses
  • Borrowing money to trade
  • Chasing losses
  • Inability to stop
  • Sleep disruption
  • Relationship conflict
  • Thoughts of self-harm
Activity 22

C. When to Stop Trading Entirely

  • Stopping entirely may be the wisest choice if:
  • Trading repeatedly causes emotional distress
  • You cannot follow your own limits
  • You hide or lie about trading
  • You chase losses
  • You trade with borrowed money or essential money
  • You feel addicted to market checking
  • Trading harms your sleep, job, family, health, or faith/community life
