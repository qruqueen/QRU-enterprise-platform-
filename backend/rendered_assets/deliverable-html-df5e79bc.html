Practice

  • Choose one AI-generated answer and verify its claims using reliable sources before accepting it.
  • Look at an AI tool you already use and identify what data it may use, what task it automates, and where human judgment is still needed.
  • Discuss a real-world AI use, such as hiring tools or medical decision support, and list possible benefits, bias risks, privacy concerns, and accountability questions.
