Today, we’re talking about something every human does, every human needs, and many of us try to negotiate with: **sleep**.

[0:20–0:45] Episode Question

**HOST:**

Today’s question is simple:

**Why do humans need sleep, and can rest replace it?**

The short answer is: **sleep is biologically essential.**

It supports your brain, immune system, heart, metabolism, emotions, and physical recovery.

Rest is valuable too. Rest can help reduce fatigue, lower stress, and give your body and mind a break in the moment.

But rest does **not** replace the biological functions of adequate sleep over time.

So let’s make that real.
